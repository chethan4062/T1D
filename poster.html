Type 1, explained

Know the four signs of type 1 diabetes

Type 1 can start at any age and come on over a few weeks. In young children it can be much faster.

  • ToiletPeeing a lot, getting up in the night, or bedwetting in a child who was dry.
  • ThirstyA thirst that drinking doesn't fix.
  • TiredWiped out for no reason, with no energy for normal things.
  • ThinnerLosing weight without trying.

Seen any of these? Ask a doctor for a test today.A finger-prick blood test or a urine test takes minutes. You don't need all four signs.

Go to emergency care now if they alsoare being sick, have tummy pain, are breathing fast and deep, have breath that smells fruity, or are very sleepy or confused.
